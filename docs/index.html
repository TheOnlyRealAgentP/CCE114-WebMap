    About this map

    HUC8 subbasins (Watershed Boundary Dataset) and US state boundaries, drawn over a switchable basemap. Hover any basin for its name, code, area and states; click to pin it. Red points are 2026 flood events — click one for its full report.

    Flood coverage runs 1 Jan – 31 Jul 2026. NOAA publishes the Storm Events Database on roughly a three-month lag, so later events are not in it yet. That window holds 3,971 floods, 39 deaths and $1.4 billion in damage.

    Floods: NOAA NCEI Storm Events Database. A point marks where the event began; damage figures are the reporting office's estimate, not an audited loss.

    Boundaries: USGS WBD, simplified for the web — use the source data for analysis, not these outlines. States: US Census TIGER (1:20m).
